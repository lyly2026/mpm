Understanding Fourier Transform

What is it? The Fourier Transform is a fundamental mathematical function that converts a signal from its original domain (usually time or space) into the Frequency Domain.

f(t) = ∑ [ A_k * sin(2π * f_k * t + φ_k) ]

1. Signal Decomposition: Any complex waveform can be broken down into an exact sum of pure sine waves with different frequencies, amplitudes, and phases.

2. Epicycles & Complex Numbers: In 2D space, Fourier series represent paths as sum of rotating complex vectors ($e^{i \omega t}$). Connecting these rotating circles end-to-end forms epicycles that can trace any arbitrary continuous drawing!
