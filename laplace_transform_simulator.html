Laplace Transform Fundamentals

Definition: The bilateral Laplace Transform converts a continuous-time signal $f(t)$ into a complex frequency domain function $F(s)$:

F(s) = ∫ [ f(t) e^(-st) dt ] from -∞ to +∞

Where $s = \sigma + j\omega$ is the complex frequency parameter.

Poles (X): Values of $s$ where $|H(s)| \to \infty$. Poles determine system natural frequencies and exponential growth/decay rates.

Zeros (O): Values of $s$ where $H(s) = 0$. Zeros block or attenuate specific complex frequencies.

Stability & ROC: A LTI system is Bounded-Input Bounded-Output (BIBO) stable if and only if all poles lie in the Left-Half Plane ($\sigma < 0$).
